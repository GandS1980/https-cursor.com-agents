# YouTube + Fiverr playbook

## Intake checklist

Collect these inputs before drafting anything:
- What services are sold on Fiverr?
- Who is the ideal buyer?
- What problem is being solved?
- What proof points exist: results, years of experience, portfolio pieces, turnaround time, niche expertise?
- What tone should the channel use: expert, friendly, premium, direct, educational?
- Which language and market matter most?
- Which link should be the primary CTA?

## Channel positioning formula

Use this template:

`I help <ideal client> achieve <outcome> with <service> without <pain point>.`

Example:

`I help podcast hosts get clean, broadcast-ready audio with fast editing support without hiring a full production team.`

## About section structure

Use a short 3-part structure:
1. Who the creator helps
2. What the creator delivers
3. What action the viewer should take next

Suggested shape:
- Sentence 1: audience + outcome
- Sentence 2: services + differentiators
- Sentence 3: CTA to Fiverr or booking link

## Banner copy pattern

Keep banner copy short enough to scan quickly:
- Headline: primary value proposition
- Subheadline: service types or niche
- CTA: `Order on Fiverr`, `See portfolio`, or `Message for a custom quote`

## Video ideas by Fiverr-style service

### Design
- Logo redesign breakdown
- Before/after brand refresh
- Common mistakes in low-cost branding

### Writing
- How to brief a copywriter well
- Landing page teardown
- Before/after rewrite examples

### Video editing
- Raw footage to polished edit walkthrough
- 3 edits that increase retention
- Explainer on captions, hooks, and pacing

### Marketing
- Audit a weak funnel
- 5 mistakes in ad creatives
- Show how a campaign is improved step by step

### Virtual assistance / admin
- Workflow automation demos
- Inbox or calendar organization tips
- SOP examples for busy founders

## CTA guidance

Use only one primary CTA per profile block. Examples:
- `See my Fiverr packages:`
- `Hire me on Fiverr for this service:`
- `Need a custom version? Start here:`

Avoid overstuffing the description with multiple competing links.

## Quality check before publishing

Verify that:
- The description states the service clearly.
- The CTA matches the actual Fiverr offer.
- Claims are supportable.
- The first five video ideas connect directly to buyer pain points.
- Brand voice is consistent across banner, About text, and featured video.
